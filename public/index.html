Stripe Connect - onboarding flows

This project is for product discovery purposes only. To get access to Stripe Connect, please reach out to  Guillaume Dauphin.

Stripe-hosted onboarding

A Stripe-hosted web form with your brand's name, color, and icon. Reads the account's requirements and generates a guided flow, including document upload and real-time verification.

  • Integration effort: Minimal, go live quickly
  • Customization: Stripe-branded, limited platform branding
  • Ideal for: Platforms that want Stripe to handle onboarding

Embedded onboarding

A themeable onboarding UI embedded directly in your app, so connected accounts never leave your site. Same guided flow, document upload, and real-time verification as Stripe-hosted, with limited Stripe branding.

  • Integration effort: More effort, still go live quickly
  • Customization: Highly themeable, limited Stripe branding
  • Ideal for: Platforms that want a branded flow within their app
Work in progress

API onboarding

Build your own onboarding UI with the Accounts API. Your platform owns identity verification, localization, and error handling, and must review requirements at least every 6 months.

  • Integration effort: Most effort, can delay going live
  • Customization: Full control over your own UI
  • Ideal for: Platforms with resources to build and maintain it
